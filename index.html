Mic → whatever audio output is currently routed.

Input —
State Idle

Connect your Bluetooth speaker in your device's Settings before tapping Start. The page can't see which output is active — it just plays to whatever the OS is routing.

Keep this tab in the foreground — iOS pauses microphone capture when the tab goes to the background or the phone locks.
Expect ~150–300 ms of Bluetooth latency. That's physics, not the page.
